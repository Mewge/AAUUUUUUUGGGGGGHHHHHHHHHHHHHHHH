#2: Psychic



Why is this in the #2 spot?

Psychic might seem like an odd choice for the #2 spot -- they are incredibly weak on their own and don't have many good starting moves or synergies. However, they become extremely powerful damage dealers and support units during Mewgenics' late game. High starting mana, high mobility, and amazing supporting spells like Mind Meld earn Psychic the spot on this list.

Facts about the Psychic:
  1. Cats gain +1 Intelligence, +1 Charisma, +1 Speed, +5 Starting Mana, and lose -1 Constitution with this collar equipped
  2. Through levels 1-9, Psychics can gain Intelligence, Charisma, or Speed
  3. This class has the most Gravity Element spells
