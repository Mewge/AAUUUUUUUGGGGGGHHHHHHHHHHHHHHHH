#1: Fighter



Why is this in the #1 spot?

Fighter has the most versatile, consistent, and high-damage moveset in the entire game. When using this class in a run, you're sure to have an easier time. Similar classes would be the Butcher and Monk, but they do not compare to the absolute gigachad that is the Fighter.

Facts about the Fighter:
  1. Cats gain +2 Strength and +1 Speed and lose -1 Intelligence with this collar equipped
  2. Through levels 1-9, Fighters can gain Constitution, Strength, or Speed
  3. This class is referencing the Fighter class in Dungeons and Dragons
